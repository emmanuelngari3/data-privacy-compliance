My approach

From requirement to evidence.

A practical audit should not stop at asking whether a policy exists. It should test how the control works and what evidence supports it.

AssessUnderstand the processing activity, systems, people and existing controls.
IdentifyDocument findings and the evidence supporting them.
RecommendTranslate risks into practical and prioritised corrective actions.
